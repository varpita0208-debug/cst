# My College Using Images

Source: TASKS OF CST PDF, pages 6.

Create My College with an introduction, five courses in an unordered list, five departments in an ordered list with two or three subjects each, a facilities description list, at least four college images and a campus background. Add college website links and bookmarks for About College, Courses, Departments, Facilities and Contact.
