# Explore India Travel Website

Source: TASKS OF CST PDF, pages 7–9.

Create ExploreIndia with index.html, destinations.html, foods.html, places.html, gallery.html, contact.html and an images folder. Home needs formatted text, an image, a background and navigation. Destinations needs five destinations, five travel essentials, a nested list, three images and Back to Top. Foods needs five dishes, categories, three descriptions and three images. Places needs five places with headings, descriptions and images plus external links. Gallery needs six images grouped by theme. Contact needs email, phone and address. Connect the pages with hyperlinks.
